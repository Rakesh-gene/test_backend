# GAP-005: Trace propagation, backpressure and recovery

Recorded 7 November 2026. **005.1/005.2/005.3 are resolved for current CLI/IPC/job
boundaries**, with inspected native CI and a ten-minute Windows x64 load run.
Future native-provider callback/session continuity remains an explicit P08/P10
acceptance obligation. This does not complete the whole P12 qualification phase.

## Trace and export behavior

The CLI opens a command span and accepts bounded upstream W3C
`TRACEPARENT`/`TRACESTATE`. Authenticated IPC negotiates support; legacy peers
receive their original request shape. Async-local context isolates concurrent
requests and background jobs. Baggage is rejected and invalid headers do not
manufacture parentage. Durable activities and logs retain matching trace/span IDs.
CLI telemetry does not contaminate JSON output.

The pinned standard OpenTelemetry batch processors now receive
`selfObsMeterProvider`. Standard `otel.sdk.processor.span.*` and
`otel.sdk.processor.log.*` metrics report queue size/capacity and processed records,
including `error.type=queue_full` and export errors. Each queue holds at most 512
records; batches contain at most 64. Exporter timeout is 500 ms.

`kode status --json` and the rich CLI expose queued/dropped/failed record counts
and failed metric-export attempts separately. Local collection does not wait for
the collector and retains only its latest fixed-size summary. Counters are
cumulative for the current process, whose PID is also in status. Queue drops mean
local loss; export failure means delivery was not confirmed, not proof that every
record was rejected remotely. After recovery, cumulative loss counters are still
available through standard OTLP metrics. Disabled OTLP is labeled explicitly;
durable local events continue working.

The terminal yields after five replay pages per poll so retained history cannot
monopolize polling. Recent projected activity stays capped at 200 events, with
that count visible in the CLI.

## Crash/recovery behavior

Job descriptors persist initiating context before lease acquisition, then persist
the job span's W3C carrier and trace/span IDs before external dispatch. After a hard
runner kill, startup creates a new `workspace.job.reconcile` span under the saved
job parent. Attributes and durable activity record an unknown prior outcome,
blocked status and `replayed=false`. The original job context remains persisted.

Recovery does not recreate/end the crashed span, invent its duration/result, rerun
the command, restore an approval grant or claim an orphan process stopped. Later
restarts retain the blocked descriptor. If recovery itself crashes between audit
and descriptor publication, another actual reconciliation attempt may be audited;
neither attempt retries the external action. Legacy/invalid context is explicitly
unavailable and starts a new trace, independently of the inspecting client's trace.

This covers implemented CLI/IPC/job boundaries. Future P08/P10 native session and
callback adapters must persist context before dispatch and restore it at their own
callback/reconciliation boundaries, with provider-specific fault evidence. Kode
does not fabricate provider-internal spans or spans lost from memory in a hard kill.

## Verification

`tests/tracing.test.ts` covers isolation, bounded/invalid carriers, real CLI/IPC/job
parentage, redacted durable/log/OTLP sinks and legacy negotiation.
`tests/observability-recovery.test.ts` adds:

1. A slow real OTLP receiver, overflow of both queues, standard loss metrics,
   locally readable diagnostics, preserved durable proof metadata, redacted
   logs/exports and successful collector recovery.
2. A real runner killed after a bounded fixture command dispatches. Restart retains
   its trace and exports a distinct reconciliation span parented to the saved job
   span. Dispatch count stays one; another restart does not reconcile already
   blocked jobs again. Legacy/invalid context is unavailable. No completion span
   for the crashed job is invented.

`scripts/qualify-observability.mjs` drives the actual compiled CLI in a native PTY
with five explicitly simulated agents, 10,000 initial history events and 200
normalized durable events/second. Default duration is 600 seconds. Collector phases
are healthy, 1.5-second delayed replies, connection resets, and healthy recovery.
One extra 2,000-span synthetic telemetry burst exercises overflow separately from
the normalized-event rate. Operations also log, so log counts exceed event counts.

Assertions cover queue bounds, visible drops/failures, resumed exports, durable
event accounting, exactly one durable decision per approval exercised through the
PTY, capped projected activity, and key-to-visible/ping p95 at or below 250 ms.
Ten-minute qualification must achieve at least 190 observed events/second (5%
tolerance around the 200/s target). CI runs explicitly labeled ten-second native
regressions; these do not substitute for ten-minute qualification.
